# Unit Economics & Financial Modelling

## 1. Value of a Converted Lead to Credex
When a high-savings lead converts through the SpendPilot audit engine into an active Credex client, the monetary value is substantial. 

An average converted mid-market lead representing 40 developer seats spends roughly $1,500/month across their holistic AI stack ($20/seat retail for IDE access, plus direct API developer tokens). Credex moves these teams onto wholesale credit pools, saving the client 30% while retaining an institutional 15% brokerage spread on the volume.

$$\text{Monthly Gross Revenue Per Account} = \$1,500 \times 0.15 = \$225/\text{month}$$
$$\text{Annual Contract Value (ACV)} = \$225 \times 12 = \$2,700/\text{year}$$
Assuming a conservative Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) duration of 3 years:
$$\text{Total Account LTV} = \$2,700 \times 3 = \$8,100$$

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Modeling
Because our Go-To-Market distribution map is entirely organic, early-stage CAC is highly optimized:
* **Direct Organic Engineering DMs:** Requiring exclusively developer sweat equity and time. 100 outbound interactions yield 15 audits, resulting in 6 leads and 1 closed client. Net customer acquisition cost tracks at **$0 direct spend**.
* **VC Portfolio Program Partnership:** Investing time into pitch presentations for managing partners. Securing one VC firm unlocks access to 50 portfolio companies, converting roughly 5 accounts. Total operational cost evaluates to **$0 acquisition capital layout**.

## 4. The Path to $1,000,000 ARR in 18 Months
To scale SpendPilot into a mechanism generating $1 Million in Annual Recurring Revenue ($83,333 Run-Rate per month) within an 18-month timeline, the system requires the following parameters to be true:

* **Active Closed Client Volume Needed:** $$\frac{\$83,333\text{ target revenue per month}}{\$225\text{ gross margin per client per month}} = 371\text{ active accounts required.}$$
* **Monthly Acquisition Velocity:** To hit 371 active accounts in 18 months, Credex must close an average of **21 new mid-market accounts every month**.
* **Traffic Inflow Ingestion Required:** Working backward from our conversion metrics, to close 21 clients monthly, the top-of-funnel must consistently ingest:
  $$\frac{21\text{ clients}}{0.0032\text{ funnel conversion yield}} = 6,562\text{ unique, high-intent targeted visitors per month.}$$

This is easily achievable by turning the platform into a viral benchmark standard within the startup tech ecosystem, scaling programmatic integrations with early-stage venture capital accelerators, and maintaining our content-driven inbound distribution playbooks.
