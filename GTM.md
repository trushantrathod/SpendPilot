# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. The Target Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
SpendPilot is not pitched vaguely to "startups"[cite: 170]. Our hyper-focused primary target user is the **Head of Engineering, VPE (Vice President of Engineering), or technical CTO** at **Seed to Series A software companies (team size 15 to 60 employees)**[cite: 170]. 

At this specific growth inflection point, companies have recently completed venture capital funding rounds, scaled developer headcount aggressively, and blanketed the team with overlapping developer tool seat licenses (Cursor, Copilot, Claude)[cite: 45, 46, 47]. The engineering leadership wants to reduce operational capital waste but lacks the time to manually benchmark their subscriptions[cite: 27, 29].

## 2. High-Intent Trigger States & Search Vectors
Our target profile encounters specific systemic events that drive them to actively seek out spend optimization assets[cite: 171]:
* **The "End of Month Invoice Shock":** Occurs when direct OpenAI/Anthropic API utilization invoices spike 300% due to an unoptimized developer loops, forcing them to look for alternative structures[cite: 49, 50].
* **Active Information-Seeking Queries:** The target persona types high-intent strings into technical forums: *"Cursor Business vs GitHub Copilot pricing data 2026"*, *"Is Claude Team plan worth it for 20 devs"*, or *"How to cut down OpenAI token spending overhead"*[cite: 45, 46, 47, 48, 50, 171].

We will seed the tool directly inside organic technical communities where technical founders and engineering managers naturally engage[cite: 172]:
