# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## 1. The Target Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
SpendPilot is not pitched vaguely to "startups." Our hyper-focused primary target user is the **Head of Engineering, VPE (Vice President of Engineering), or technical CTO** at **Seed to Series A software companies (team size 15 to 60 employees)**.

At this specific growth inflection point, companies have recently completed venture capital funding rounds, scaled developer headcount aggressively, and blanketed the team with overlapping developer tool seat licenses (Cursor, Copilot, Claude).

The engineering leadership wants to reduce operational capital waste but lacks the time to manually benchmark their subscriptions.

## 2. High-Intent Trigger States & Search Vectors
Our target profile encounters specific systemic events that drive them to actively seek out spend optimization assets:

* **The "End of Month Invoice Shock":** Occurs when direct OpenAI/Anthropic API utilization invoices spike 300% due to unoptimized developer loops, forcing them to look for alternative structures.
* **Active Information-Seeking Queries:** The target persona types high-intent strings into technical forums:
  * "Cursor Business vs GitHub Copilot pricing data 2026"
  * "Is Claude Team plan worth it for 20 devs"
  * "How to cut down OpenAI token spending overhead"

## 3. High-Density Distribution Hubs
We will seed the tool directly inside organic technical communities where technical founders and engineering managers naturally engage:

* **Subreddits:** `r/startups`, `r/softwareengineering`, `r/YCombinator`, and `r/DevOps`
* **Vibrant Slack/Discord Nodes:** *SaaStr Community*, *IndieHackers Global*, *YCombinator Founder Slack*, and *The CTO Craft network*
* **Curated X Lists:** Curated algorithmic lists focusing heavily on active SaaS engineering builders and venture-backed technical leads

## 4. Bootstrapping the First 100 Users ($0 Budget)
We will secure our initial acquisition layer within 30 days using targeted, non-spammy organic outreach:

* **The "Public Audit Roast" Content Blueprint:** We will scrape top trending software products currently launching on Product Hunt or YC Directory, run their estimated development team sizing through SpendPilot, and post an informational cost-breakdown thread on X tagging the founder. Highlighting how they can immediately claw back $1,500/mo creates an instant viral loop where other founders request an audit link.

* **Value-First Direct Engineering DMs:** Find technical leads on LinkedIn/X who are openly hiring developers. Send a crisp, zero-fluff direct message:

> "Hey [Name], noticed you're scaling your engineering team. Built a quick tool that checks if your developers are double-provisioned on overlapping Cursor and Claude licenses. Completely free, no login required. Might save you a grand on your monthly run-rate. Let me know what you think."

## 5. Our Unfair Distribution Channel
The ultimate distribution advantage belongs to Credex's primary data ecosystem. Credex sits on the sell-side data layer of distressed, over-forecasted corporate cloud credits.

We can partner directly with early-stage venture capital firms (e.g., Techstars, YC alumni networks) to pitch SpendPilot as a complimentary, value-add auditing service for their entire active portfolio, establishing instant high-trust access to hundreds of high-growth companies overnight.
